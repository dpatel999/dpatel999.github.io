Off Magento, without dropping the year

A Canadian food brand on an aging stack, selling at home and starting to ship to the US. Organic search is a real channel.

  • Shopify Markets for the US
  • Checkout rule for a regional fee

03 · Wholesale

PDF price lists, retired

A distributor whose reps still send spreadsheets. DTC exists and must not see wholesale prices. An ERP is real, and it is phase two.

  • B2B companies and catalogs
  • DTC wall so prices do not leak
  • A written line for what the ERP will own

$40k–$75k CAD · 12 weeks

04 · Retail

The counter and the site disagree

A multi-location retailer. Endless aisle is a slogan. Staff need a deposit and a ship-from-store flow the default tiles do not have.

  • POS UI extensions
  • One inventory story
  • A floor test before a Saturday

$28k–$52k CAD · 10 weeks

05 · Service

The order is a work order

Jewellery repair, alterations, or any job that starts at the counter and ends as a tracked piece of work — not a normal SKU sale.

  • POS intake
  • A private app for status
  • Customer-facing updates that are not a shared inbox

$22k–$48k CAD · 8–12 weeks

06 · After a bad handoff

The last agency left the repo

The store is live. Nobody wants to touch it. You do not need a rebrand. You need an adult who will read the theme before rewriting it.

  • A short list of what is actually unsafe
  • A retainer or a fixed repair phase

$4k–$8k, then a phase CAD · 2–3 weeks to the document
