Questions worth answering in public

How much does a Shopify agency cost in Canada?+–

For a senior studio in 2026, read $18k–$45k for a custom storefront, $28k–$80k for a migration, $40k–$120k for a real Plus implementation, and $4.5k–$18k a month for a retainer. Freelancers sit under that. Platinum programs sit well above it, often in USD. The pricing page is the longer version, and a written scope replaces it.

Do you only work in Canada?+–

Canada is the market we are built for. US brands can start a scope now. We will not invent a New York office to look larger. The studio day is Eastern Time, and ranges are in CAD unless we agree otherwise.

Are you a Shopify Plus Partner?+–

Not claimed. A partner badge is a directory status, not a skill, and this site will not wear one it has not earned. The work — checkout extensibility, Markets, B2B, POS, custom apps — does not wait on the badge.

Theme, Plus, or Hydrogen?+–

Theme, until a constraint proves otherwise. Plus when checkout, markets, or B2B are the actual problem. Hydrogen when Liquid cannot hold the experience and the revenue can pay for a second product. There is a full note under Insights.

Who does the work?+–

The person who scopes it stays on it. Specialists come in for QA, photography, or copy. We do not staff a pitch with seniors and the build with people you have not met.

Can you sign an NDA and work in our repo?+–

Yes. The theme, the app, and the admin stay yours. We do not hold the store hostage at the end of a contract.

Bring the constraint. Leave with a range.
