Shopify agency pricing in Canada, without the theatre

Most sites will not publish a number. The number still exists. Here is the band a Canadian brand should walk into a sales call already knowing.

Hourly, if you insist

Public 2026 ranges, mostly quoted in USD and broadly true in CAD at par for senior Canadian work: boutique and fractional help around $100–$175 an hour, a certified Plus partner around $175–$300, premier and platinum specialists around $250–$400. Clew prices phases, not a blurred rack rate. When a phase does not fit, senior time sits in the middle of that certified band.

Projects

  • Custom storefront — $18k–$45k. Higher if the catalogue is hostile or a second language is in the first release.
  • Migration — $28k–$80k. Redirects and a rehearsal are inside that, not a change order.
  • Plus implementation — $40k–$120k. Buying the Plus plan is extra, and it is Shopify's invoice, not ours.
  • Custom app or checkout extension — $15k–$60k for one job. A platform fantasy is a different conversation.
  • POS — $18k–$55k including a floor test. Hardware is yours to buy.
  • B2B — $30k–$90k before an ERP phase. The ERP is named separately or it will haunt the budget.
  • Hydrogen — $70k–$160k, and the second year is a retainer whether anyone put it on the slide.

Retainers

Light fractional help in this market is published as low as a few thousand a month. A real Plus retainer at an established partner is often $10k–$15k. Multi-workstream enterprise retainers run $20k–$30k. Clew's senior retainer is $4.5k–$18k a month depending on revenue and how many surfaces are in motion, 90 days minimum, then month to month.

What moves the number

  • Catalogue quality. Dirty data is a project.
  • A second country or a second language in the first release.
  • Checkout rules, subscriptions, or B2B companies.
  • Anything that must talk to an ERP, a 3PL with opinions, or a custom wholesale tool.
  • A deadline inside eight weeks. Speed is a scope cut or a premium, never free.

What should not be hiding in it

App subscriptions, product photography, copywriting, paid media, Shopify's own plan fees, and your staff's time to make decisions. If a quote is silent on those, ask. Silence is how a $40k build becomes a $70k one in month three.

A range is not a quote. It is the number that keeps the first call honest.
