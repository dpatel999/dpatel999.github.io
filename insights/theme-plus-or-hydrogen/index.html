Theme, Plus, or Hydrogen

You do not need a workshop to make this call. You need the constraint, the revenue, and a willingness to leave a fashionable option on the table.

Stay on a theme when

  • The pain is merchandising, speed, or a homepage that cannot tell the truth about the product.
  • Your team should launch a collection without a frontend deploy.
  • Revenue is under about $5M and nothing structural is broken. Hydrogen will not outsell a clearer product page.

A custom theme is still real engineering. It is just the engineering that matches the job. Budget $18k–$45k and six to ten weeks for a first release that your marketers can run.

Move to Plus when

  • Checkout has a rule you are faking with an app and a prayer.
  • A second country needs duties, currency, and a catalogue that is not a copy-paste.
  • Wholesale companies need their own prices on the same store as DTC.
  • You are above the line where Shopify's Plus fee is smaller than the workaround.

Plus without a checkout, markets, or B2B problem is a logo. Do the math on Shopify's plan against the cost of the workaround before you take a meeting about 'enterprise'.

Go Hydrogen when

  • The experience is a configurator, a content world, or a product that happens to sell — not a catalogue with a nicer font.
  • You can staff or retain the frontend after launch. Headless is a second product.
  • The revenue can pay $70k–$160k without betting the year on it.

If you are under about $5M and someone is pitching Hydrogen as modernization, ask them to price the theme that solves the actual sentence. If they will not, you have learned something useful about the pitch.

Default to the theme. Promote Plus when the constraint is commercial. Promote Hydrogen when Liquid has actually lost.

Clew will write that choice down in a diagnosis if you do not want to make it alone. Two to three weeks, credited if a build follows.
